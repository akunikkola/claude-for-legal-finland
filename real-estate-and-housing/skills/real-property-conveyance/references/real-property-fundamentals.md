# Fundamentals of real property and housing law

**Confirmed from the source (Finlex, 2026-06):** the statute numbers listed below and the key
concepts of the Code of Real Estate (public purchase witness, electronic conveyancing system,
registration of title, mortgage, quality / dominion / legal defect) have been checked in Finlex.
Section-level content, time limits and administrative practice are always checked at the time of use
with the `legal-core:legal-research` skill.

## Key statutes

- **Code of Real Estate (maakaari 540/1995, "MK")** — sale, exchange and gift of real property,
  registration matters (registration of title, special rights, mortgages), and liability for
  defects.
- **Housing Transactions Act (asuntokauppalaki 843/1994, "AsKL")** — sale of housing-company
  shares: the construction phase (the RS system), sale of a new dwelling, sale of a used dwelling,
  liability for defects.
- **Limited Liability Housing Companies Act (asunto-osakeyhtiölaki 1599/2009, "AOYL")** —
  governance of the housing company, maintenance liability, alterations, charges, and the
  shareholder's legal protection.
- **Act on Residential Leases (laki asuinhuoneiston vuokrauksesta 481/1995, "AHVL")** — residential
  tenancies; contains mandatory provisions protecting the tenant.
- **Act on Commercial Leases (laki liikehuoneiston vuokrauksesta 482/1995, "LHVL")** — business
  premises; non-mandatory to a greater extent.
- **Act on the Brokerage of Real Estate and Rental Apartments (laki kiinteistöjen ja
  vuokrahuoneistojen välityksestä 1074/2000)** — the estate agent's duties and liability.
- **Real Estate Formation Act (kiinteistönmuodostamislaki 554/1995)** — parcelling, easements and
  cadastral procedures.
- Related: land use planning and building permits (`environment-and-planning`), the transfer tax
  act (varainsiirtoverolaki 931/1996) (`taxation`), and the Consumer Protection Act between a
  trader and a consumer.

## Real property or housing-company shares — the decisive distinction

| | Real property | Housing-company shares |
|---|---|---|
| Object of the sale | the land with its buildings (or a parcel or undivided share) | shares in a limited company that entitle the holder to possession of a flat |
| Applicable act | the Code of Real Estate | the Housing Transactions Act (plus the Limited Liability Housing Companies Act for the company relationship) |
| Form | **prescribed form**: a written deed of sale plus a public purchase witness, or the electronic conveyancing system of Maanmittauslaitos | no prescribed form (in practice written) |
| Registration | registration of title at Maanmittauslaitos within the time limit | entry in the share register / the housing information system (Maanmittauslaitos) |
| Security | mortgage plus mortgage deed | pledge of the share certificate or of the electronic share |
| Taxes | transfer tax (rates from the source) | transfer tax (a different rate — from the source) |

Hybrid forms: a real estate company (mutual or ordinary — different rules), a house on a leased plot
(the building plus the leasehold), part-ownership and right-of-occupancy dwellings — identify these
and flag them.

## Types of defect in a sale

- **Code of Real Estate**: a quality defect (physical condition, including a latent defect), a
  defect in dominion (the planning or permit situation differs from what was stated), a legal
  defect (an encumbrance on ownership or a pledge) — the concepts are confirmed from the Act; the
  conditions and the remedies from the source.
- **Housing Transactions Act**: the general provision on defects, a defect in the information
  given, and an economic defect (the company's finances or liabilities are worse than stated) — the
  rules for new and for used dwellings differ.
- **Scale of remedies**: price reduction, damages, rescission of the sale (a material defect) — the
  threshold for rescission is high.

## Periods for notice of defect and for liability

Notice of a defect **within a reasonable time** of discovery, plus an absolute long-stop period (the
periods for real property and for a dwelling differ — **retrieve the periods in force from the
source**, not from memory). The seller's gross negligence or conduct contrary to good faith may
break the long-stop period — the conditions from the source.

## Actors and registers

- **Maanmittauslaitos (MML)** — the register of titles and mortgages, the real estate register, the
  housing information system, the electronic conveyancing system, and cadastral procedures.
- **Public purchase witnesses (kaupanvahvistajat)** — confirmation of a sale of real property.
- **The manager / the board of the housing company** — the manager's certificate
  (isännöitsijäntodistus) is the single most important source of information in a sale of
  housing-company shares.
- **Kuluttajariitalautakunta** — consumer disputes about dwellings and brokerage (recommendations).

## List of documents to obtain (the skill cannot see the registers)

Real property: certificate of title, certificate of encumbrances, extract from the real estate
register, extract from the town plan and building permits, leases. Housing-company shares: the
manager's certificate with its annexes, the articles of association, the financial statements, the
maintenance needs assessment and the renovations carried out or decided on, and the energy
certificate. Draw up the list for a human to obtain — do not assume the contents.

## Typical risk points

- The wrong body of rules (real property versus housing-company shares).
- A defect of form in a sale of real property or in a pre-contract.
- A notice of defect that is late or not specific.
- Out-of-date information in the manager's certificate; an upcoming pipe renovation.
- The redemption terms and rent increases of a leased plot.
- Mentions of a "need for further investigation" in a condition survey report that were not
  followed up → the buyer's duty to investigate.
