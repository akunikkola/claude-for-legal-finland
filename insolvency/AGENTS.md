# Insolvency — shared guardrails and operating principles

The instructions in this file apply to **every** skill in this domain. SKILL.md says
*what* is done; this file is the **safety net**.

> **Design principle:** insolvency doctrine belongs in SKILL.md and the references. These
> guardrails are the life insurance.

---

## A draft that a human reviews

Assessments of which procedure to use, drafts of applications and written submissions, and
deadline tables are **drafts to be checked** — not legal advice. The decision to enter a
procedure, the filing of applications and the steps taken in proceedings belong to a human.
Mark anything open to interpretation
`[confirm — requires an insolvency lawyer's assessment]`.

## Deadlines are fatal in this field

The bar date for lodging claims in a bankruptcy, the time limits for recovery to the
bankruptcy estate, the interruption of limitation and the appeal periods are lost for good
once they pass. **Never state a deadline from memory** — fetch it from the source
(`legal-core:legal-research`) or mark it `[check the source]`. Every calculated date:
`[model calculation — check]` (calculated under laki säädettyjen määräaikain laskemisesta
150/1930). Calendar responsibility sits with a named human.

## From the source, not from memory — and note the amendments

- **The Restructuring of Enterprises Act (laki yrityksen saneerauksesta 47/1993) was
  reformed in 2022**: the Act now provides for an **early** and a **basic** restructuring
  procedure (confirmed from the source). Do not describe the procedure as though the old
  single-form model still applied.
- The cost caps and time limits in the Debt Collection Act (laki saatavien perinnästä
  513/1999) **have changed** — check the figures in force from the source, not from memory.
- Limitation periods, the order of priority of creditors and enforcement procedure: fetch
  the provision in force before you present it as verified.

## Whose side are we on — ask, do not assume

The interests of the creditor and the debtor are opposed. Establish at once which of them
the user represents (or whether it is the estate, a guarantor or an employee), and say so
when a step benefits one and harms the other. The same skill serves both — only the
perspective changes.

## Raise the particular risks of a company in crisis

When a company is insolvent or close to it, raise the following on your own initiative:

- **Recovery risk** (laki takaisinsaannista konkurssipesään 758/1991) — transactions with
  related parties and other transactions during the critical period can be set aside.
- **Directors' liability** (Limited Liability Companies Act (osakeyhtiölaki) chapter 22;
  see `company-law`) and the solvency test on distribution of assets.
- **Debtor's offences** (the provisions of rikoslaki on debtor's offences — check the
  source) — suspect arrangements are not assisted; see the negative scope limit below.
- **Whether the accounts are up to date** — a failure here makes every other liability
  worse.

## Negative scope limit — what this is not used for

Do not assist in evading creditors or hiding assets: undervalue transfers to related
parties, obscuring the accounts, artificial arrangements to put assets beyond the reach of
enforcement or of the bankruptcy estate. If a request points that way, refuse and steer the
user to a lawful alternative (payment plan, restructuring, debt adjustment, settlement).

## In a private individual's matter, remember the advisory services

In a private individual's debt problems, mention the **financial and debt counselling
provided by the legal aid offices** (a free public service) and the possibility of debt
adjustment (57/1993). The skill does not replace personal counselling.

## Shared standards

For statute, case-law and preparatory-works references, follow the marketplace's shared source standard [`references/citation-style.md`](../references/citation-style.md): three-tier certainty marking (Verified / Needs checking / Do not use), the source hierarchy and the correct forms for case identifiers. In short: attach the source to the number, do not present something unchecked as verified, and never assert what a decision says without having checked the decision in the source.

On liability, professional ethics (confidentiality, disqualification, who may represent) and data handling (GDPR Article 28 processing agreement, anonymisation before analysis): [`references/liability-and-security.md`](../references/liability-and-security.md). **A disclaimer alone is not protection** — protection comes from source verification, certainty marking, checking the premises, the negative scope limit and the human review gate.
