# Fundamentals of insolvency law

**Confirmed from the source (Finlex, 2026-06):** the statute numbers named below have
been checked in Finlex, as has the fact that the Restructuring of Enterprises Act
contains the early and basic restructuring procedures introduced in the 2022 reform.
Content at section level, the conditions and the time limits are always checked at the
moment of use with the `legal-core:legal-research` skill.

## Key statutes

- **Bankruptcy Act (konkurssilaki 120/2004)** — the conditions for bankruptcy,
  applying, administration of the estate, lodging of claims, distribution.
- **Restructuring of Enterprises Act (laki yrityksen saneerauksesta 47/1993)** —
  restructuring of an enterprise; contains both the **early** and the **basic**
  restructuring procedure (2022 reform, confirmed).
- **Act on the Adjustment of the Debts of a Private Individual (laki yksityishenkilön
  velkajärjestelystä 57/1993)** — debt adjustment and payment programme for a natural
  person.
- **Enforcement Code (ulosottokaari 705/2007)** — individual enforcement.
- **Act on the Limitation of Debts (laki velan vanhentumisesta 728/2003)** —
  limitation periods and interruption.
- **Debt Collection Act (laki saatavien perinnästä 513/1999)** — good collection
  practice, regulation of costs.
- **Act on the Order of Priority of Creditors (laki velkojien
  maksunsaantijärjestyksestä 1578/1992)** — priorities in bankruptcy and in
  enforcement.
- **Act on the Recovery of Assets to a Bankruptcy Estate (laki takaisinsaannista
  konkurssipesään 758/1991)** — setting aside transactions.
- Related: the provisions of rikoslaki on debtor's offences, the rules on pay
  security, and the management's liability and the solvency test under the Limited
  Liability Companies Act (osakeyhtiölaki 624/2006).

## Basic concepts

- **Insolvency** (maksukyvyttömyys) — the debtor is, otherwise than temporarily,
  unable to pay its debts as they fall due. Distinguish **temporary late payment**
  (liquidity) from permanent insolvency, and both from **over-indebtedness**
  (ylivelkaisuus, debts exceed assets) — the concepts play different roles in
  different procedures; the exact definitions from the source.
- **General enforcement versus individual enforcement** — bankruptcy, restructuring
  and debt adjustment concern the debtor's entire property and the creditors
  collectively; enforcement collects an individual claim.
- **Equality of creditors** — the core principle of insolvency procedures: creditors
  in the same position are treated equally (the order of priority of creditors
  1578/1992 determines the priorities).

## Map of procedures

| Procedure | For whom | Aim | Act |
|---|---|---|---|
| Voluntary arrangement / settlement | everyone | payment schedule or composition without a court | law of contract |
| Early restructuring procedure | company, **threat** of insolvency | rehabilitation before the crisis deepens | 47/1993 |
| Basic restructuring procedure | company, including one already insolvent | rehabilitation of a viable business, restructuring programme | 47/1993 |
| Bankruptcy | company and natural person | realisation of the assets and distribution to the creditors | 120/2004 |
| Debt adjustment of a private individual | natural person | payment programme and discharge of the remaining debts | 57/1993 |
| Enforcement | a single creditor | enforcement of an individual claim | 705/2007 |

The procedures interlock: an application for restructuring and a bankruptcy petition
may be pending at the same time (questions of precedence from the source); a failed
restructuring often ends in bankruptcy; a bankruptcy may **lapse** for want of assets
or continue as **public receivership** (julkisselvitys).

## The critical period and recovery to the estate

Recovery (758/1991) allows transactions made before the bankruptcy to be set aside in
favour of the estate (gifts, undervalue transfers and payments favouring a creditor
among others). The **time limits for setting aside are counted backwards from the
reference date** and are longer for transactions between related parties — fetch the
periods and the conditions from the source. The practical consequence: exceptional
payments and transfers by a company in crisis are a risk both for the recipient of the
payment and for the management.

## The order of priority of creditors (1578/1992)

The main rule is that creditors rank equally; the exceptions laid down in the Act
(including the position of creditors holding a pledge or a floating charge, the
position of restructuring debts, and subordinated claims) are checked from the source
before any forecast of distribution is given.

## Limitation in a nutshell (728/2003)

- The general limitation period is **three years**, and it can be **interrupted** (a
  reminder, a payment, an acknowledgement, a legal measure) — a new period starts from
  the interruption. The periods after a judgment, the final periods and the period for
  which an enforcement order remains enforceable: check them from the source.
- Managing limitation is the creditor's responsibility: document the acts of
  interruption and their dates.

## Authorities and actors

- **Courts** — bankruptcy and restructuring matters are heard in the käräjäoikeus
  (concentrated in certain käräjäoikeus courts — check the competent court from the
  source).
- **Konkurssiasiamies** (the Bankruptcy Ombudsman) — supervises the lawfulness of the
  administration of estates; public receiverships.
- **Ulosottolaitos** (the National Enforcement Authority) — enforcement matters.
- **Financial and debt counselling at the legal aid offices** — free help for private
  individuals.
- The **Kosti** e-services system for bankruptcy and restructuring matters (estate
  administrators, creditors).
