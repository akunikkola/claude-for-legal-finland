# Environment and planning

Finnish environmental and planning law: environmental permits and supervision, land use
planning and building, and environmental liabilities in transactions – in line with the
reformed legislation (rakentamislaki 751/2023, luonnonsuojelulaki 9/2023) and the new set
of authorities (Lupa- ja valvontavirasto 2026).

> **Drafts and analyses are to be checked – not legal advice.**
> This field is full of recent wholesale reforms in which the model's memory fails –
> statute names, authorities and thresholds are always checked from the source.
> See [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md).

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| **environmental-permits-and-supervision** | Environmental Protection Act (ympäristönsuojelulaki 527/2014): assessing whether an activity needs a permit, framing a permit application, permit consideration and permit conditions, the link to EIA (252/2017), supervision and administrative enforcement, and participation (objections, appeals). Includes the reference on the fundamentals of environmental law. |
| **land-use-planning-and-construction** | Land Use Act (alueidenkäyttölaki 132/1999, formerly MRL) and the Building Act (rakentamislaki 751/2023): the planning system and influencing planning, the new building permit and its thresholds, derogation and appeals. |
| **environmental-liability** | Environmental liabilities as a risk and in transactions: the chain of liability for contaminated soil (YSL), compensation for environmental damage (737/1994), criminal environmental liability and environmental due diligence in a corporate acquisition. |

## Statutes verified as the basis

Verified from the source (Finlex, 2026-06): the **Environmental Protection Act
(ympäristönsuojelulaki 527/2014)**, the **Land Use Act (alueidenkäyttölaki 132/1999)** –
the new name of the old MRL – the **Building Act (rakentamislaki 751/2023)** with its
permit system (rakentamislupa, purkamislupa, maisematyölupa, sijoittamislupa, climate
report), the **Nature Conservation Act (luonnonsuojelulaki 9/2023)** (which replaced
1096/1996), the **Water Act (vesilaki 587/2011)**, the **Waste Act (jätelaki 646/2011)**,
the **Act on Environmental Impact Assessment Procedure (laki ympäristövaikutusten
arviointimenettelystä 252/2017)** and the **Act on Compensation for Environmental Damage
(laki ympäristövahinkojen korvaamisesta 737/1994)**. The authorities: **Lupa- ja
valvontavirasto (LVV)** began on 1 January 2026 and replaced the AVI agencies, the ELY
centres and Valvira (confirmed from government and LVV sources).

  transaction.
- **`public-procurement` domain** – environmental considerations in procurement.
