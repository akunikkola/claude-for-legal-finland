# Environment and planning — shared guardrails and operating principles

The instructions in this file apply to **every** skill in this domain. SKILL.md says
*what* is done; this file is the **safety net**.

> **Design principle:** environmental law doctrine belongs in SKILL.md and the references.
> These guardrails are the life insurance.

---

## A draft that a human reviews

Drafts of permit applications, objections, statements and appeals, and analyses of
liability, are **drafts to be checked** — not legal advice. Applications are filed by the
operator, who also carries the burden of investigation; the technical and scientific
studies (emissions, noise, natural values) are made by a specialist in the field, not by
this domain. Mark anything open to interpretation
`[confirm — requires an environmental lawyer's assessment]`.

## The legislation and the authorities have just been reformed — memory fails here

This field is full of recent wholesale reforms. The following have been **confirmed from
the source (2026-06)** — do not use outdated names or numbers:

- **The Building Act is 751/2023** and the old maankäyttö- ja rakennuslaki (132/1999) is
  now called **alueidenkäyttölaki** (the Land Use Act). Do not refer to "maankäyttö- ja
  rakennuslaki" as the name in force. The new permit system: **rakentamislupa** (building
  permit, which replaced the building permit and the action permit), purkamislupa
  (demolition permit), maisematyölupa (landscape work permit), sijoittamislupa (siting
  permit).
- **The Nature Conservation Act is 9/2023** — it replaced the old luonnonsuojelulaki
  (1096/1996). Do not refer to the old one as being in force.
- **Lupa- ja valvontavirasto (LVV, lvv.fi) began on 1 January 2026** — it replaced the
  regional state administrative agencies (AVI), the ELY centres and Valvira. Do not direct
  the user to AVI or to an ELY centre as the authority in force; the State's environmental
  permit, supervision, water, nature conservation and EIA tasks sit with LVV. The
  transitional provisions for matters already pending are checked from the source.
- The special arrangements for appeals (including the concentration of environmental and
  water matters in the Vaasa Administrative Court) and the rights of appeal are checked
  from the source — the reform may affect the routes.

## From the source, not from memory

Check the provisions with the **`legal-core:legal-research` skill** (oik.ai/Finlex): the
thresholds for permit obligation (the list of installations in the Environmental Protection
Act), the grounds for permit conditions, the periods for participation and objections, and
the appeal periods and rights of appeal. The case law of KHO and of the Vaasa
Administrative Court is fetched from the source with its identifiers.

## Time limits and windows for participation

The periods for public notice, the deadlines for objections and opinions, and the appeal
periods are absolute — a missed window for participation cannot be recovered. Every date
`[model calculation — check]` and calendar responsibility with a human →
`legal-core:engagement-intake` (deadline scan).

## EU law is always present here

Environmental law is EU-driven: directives (industrial emissions, water framework, habitats
and birds, EIA) and the case law of the Court of Justice affect interpretation directly.
A Natura 2000 assessment and species protection can stop a project regardless of the
national permits — raise the EU level, do not treat the matter as purely national.

## Negative scope limit

Do not assist in circumventing environmental obligations: concealing emissions, flattering
monitoring results, splitting an activity that requires a permit so as to fall below the
permit threshold, or withholding information about contamination in a sale. Refuse, and
steer the user to the lawful route (amendment of the permit, notification to the
supervisory authority, prior consultation).

## Shared standards

For statute, case-law and preparatory-works references, follow the marketplace's shared source standard [`references/citation-style.md`](../references/citation-style.md): three-tier certainty marking (Verified / Needs checking / Do not use), the source hierarchy and the correct forms for case identifiers. In short: attach the source to the number, do not present something unchecked as verified, and never assert what a decision says without having checked the decision in the source.

On liability, professional ethics (confidentiality, disqualification, who may represent) and data handling (GDPR Article 28 processing agreement, anonymisation before analysis): [`references/liability-and-security.md`](../references/liability-and-security.md). **A disclaimer alone is not protection** — protection comes from source verification, certainty marking, checking the premises, the negative scope limit and the human review gate.
