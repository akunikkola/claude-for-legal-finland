# Employment law — shared safeguards and operating principles

The instructions in this file apply to **every** skill in this domain. SKILL.md says
*what* to do; this file is the **safety net**.

> **Design principle:** employment-law doctrine belongs in SKILL.md and the references. These safeguards are life insurance.

---

## A draft that a human reviews

Draft employment contracts, termination risk assessments and procedural checklists are **drafts that need checking** — not legal advice and not a final position on lawfulness. The decision to terminate belongs to the employer; its legal assessment and 🔴 RED cases belong to an employment lawyer. Mark points open to interpretation `[confirm — requires an employment lawyer's assessment]`.

## Mandatory in the employee's favour

Employment legislation is **mandatory in the employee's favour.** Do not draft or accept a term that departs from a mandatory provision to the employee's detriment, even if the parties "agree to it". Watch in particular for: a fixed-term contract without proper grounds, an excessively wide non-compete clause, a probationary period term beyond what is permitted, and circumventing protection against termination.

## The collective agreement (TES) must be checked

Always establish the applicable **collective agreement (TES)** — generally binding (yleissitova) or normally binding. A TES sets minimum terms (pay, working time, holidays, supplements, notice periods) which cannot be departed from to the employee's detriment and which may bind more tightly than the law. Name the TES applied. If it is not known, ask — do not assume.

## From the source, not from memory — and note the amendments

Check provisions with the **`legal-core:legal-research` skill** (oik.ai/Finlex). In particular:
- **The Act on Co-operation within Undertakings is 1333/2021** (confirmed from the source) — it **replaced the old co-operation act 334/2007**. Do not cite 334/2007 as being in force.
- **The regulation of non-compete clauses was reformed in 2022** (the obligation to pay compensation) — check the provision in force in chapter 3 of the Employment Contracts Act.
- Do not state **notice periods, time limits, size thresholds or negotiation periods** from memory — retrieve them from the source. Mark anything unchecked `[check]`.

## The procedure decides — not only the grounds

In termination and in change negotiations, **the procedure is as important as the substantive grounds**: a warning and a hearing (grounds relating to the person), the obligation to offer work and to provide training (collective grounds), change negotiations before the decision, and the notice period. Correct grounds followed by the wrong procedure lead to liability in damages — check both.

## Prohibition of discrimination and special protection

Raise any possible discrimination (yhdenvertaisuuslaki, tasa-arvolaki) and specially protected groups (a person who is pregnant or on family leave, a shop steward or elected representative). In these cases the risk level rises automatically.

## Shared standards

For references to statutes, case law and preparatory works, follow the marketplace's shared source standard [`references/citation-style.md`](../references/citation-style.md): three-tier certainty marking (Verified / Needs checking / Do not use), the source hierarchy and the correct forms of case identifiers. In short: attach the source to the number, never present something unchecked as verified, and never assert what a decision says without having checked the decision from the source.

On liability, professional ethics (confidentiality, disqualification, who may represent) and data handling (GDPR Article 28 processing agreement, anonymisation before analysis): [`references/liability-and-security.md`](../references/liability-and-security.md). **A disclaimer alone is not protection** — protection comes from source verification, certainty marking, checking the premises, the negative scope and the human review gate.
