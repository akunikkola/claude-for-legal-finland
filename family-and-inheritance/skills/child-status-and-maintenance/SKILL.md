# The status and maintenance of a child — custody, contact and child maintenance

This skill structures the arrangements concerning the custody, residence,
contact and maintenance of a child from the starting point of **the best
interests of the child**. The map of family law and its concepts:
`../marriage-and-division-of-property/references/family-and-inheritance-fundamentals.md`.

> **Disclaimer:** drafts and calculations are for review — not legal advice.
> The decisive criterion is the best interests of the child, and a safety
> concern comes before any arrangement. See
> `family-and-inheritance/AGENTS.md`.

## Output language

Drafts may be produced in **English** for review, but the version actually **filed with the court
or authority must be in Finnish or Swedish** (kielilaki 423/2003; oikeudenkäymiskaari for court
documents). An English filing is not admissible. Always offer to produce the Finnish version, and
state plainly that the English text is a working translation only.

Keep the Finnish term alongside the English one for legally operative concepts on first use, for
example `notice period (irtisanomisaika)`.

## Safety first

**If domestic violence, the threat of it or any other safety concern is present,
raise it before any contact and custody arrangements.** Do not produce an
arrangement that exposes the child or a threatened parent to danger. Where
necessary, direct the user to the police, to child welfare services or to a
shelter. This is absolute — it does not bend to the wishes of either parent.

## Step 1: Parenthood established

- Has parenthood (paternity or maternity) been established? Without established
  parenthood no duty of custody and no duty of maintenance arises.
- Acknowledgement and establishment of parenthood (vanhemmuuslaki 775/2022;
  repealed the paternity act 11/2015 and the maternity act 253/2018 as of
  1 January 2023) — the investigation takes place before the lastenvalvoja
  (child welfare officer); where there is a dispute, before a court.
- This stage comes before the questions of custody and maintenance.

## Step 2: Custody and residence

1. **Form of custody**: joint custody (the main rule, where it is in the best
   interests of the child) or sole custody; the content of custody (the right to
   decide on the child's affairs). There is a possibility of ordering a division
   of duties between the custodians.
2. **The child's residence**: with which parent the child lives, or
   **alternating residence** (recognised in the statute) — assess the practical
   conditions (distance, school, the child's age and the child's own wishes
   according to that age).
3. **The best interests of the child** decide: safety, continuity, the
   importance of both parents, the child's own wishes according to the child's
   stage of development.
4. Present the arrangement as an agreement (for confirmation by the
   lastenvalvoja) or, if contested, as an application to the käräjäoikeus.

## Step 3: Right of contact

- The right of contact is **the child's right** to keep in touch with the parent
  the child does not live with, not merely the parent's right. Make it concrete:
  weekdays, weekends, holidays, public holidays, collection and return, remote
  contact.
- Supported or supervised contact in special situations.
- Giving effect to contact and its enforcement if it is breached (a procedure of
  its own — from the source).

## Step 4: Maintenance

1. **The duty to maintain**: both parents are responsible according to their
   ability; the child has a right to sufficient maintenance.
2. **Calculating child maintenance**: the child's need and each parent's ability
   to maintain, in accordance with the **Ministry of Justice guideline** —
   retrieve the guideline and the figures from the source and mark the
   assumptions; do not state euro amounts from memory.
3. **The maintenance agreement**: an agreement confirmed by the lastenvalvoja is
   enforceable; an unconfirmed one is not. Index adjustment (the maintenance
   index) and the conditions for varying it.
4. **Maintenance allowance** (Kela) separately, where maintenance is not received
   — a public benefit, check with Kela.

## What this skill does NOT do

- **Does not set aside a safety concern** for the sake of any objective in an
  arrangement.
- **Does not calculate child maintenance or state euro amounts from memory** —
  the Ministry of Justice guideline and `[check]`.
- **Does not confirm agreements** — confirmation is given by the lastenvalvoja or
  the käräjäoikeus.
- **Does not produce an arrangement that serves only one parent** at the expense
  of the best interests of the child.
- **Does not replace the lastenvalvoja, child welfare services or the court.**
