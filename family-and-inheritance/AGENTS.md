# Family and inheritance law — shared guardrails and operating principles

The instructions in this file apply to **every** skill in this domain. SKILL.md says
*what* to do; this file is the **safety net**.

> **Design principle:** family and inheritance doctrine belongs in SKILL.md and the references. These guardrails are life insurance.

---

## A draft that a human reviews

Prenuptial agreements, deeds of division, wills, deeds of estate distribution, custody
and maintenance agreements and continuing powers of attorney are **drafts for review**
— not legal advice. Confirmation or the decision itself comes from the competent body:
the käräjäoikeus, the lastenvalvoja, the Digital and Population Data Services Agency
(DVV), the estate distributor or the kaupanvahvistaja. A human signs and files the
document. Mark anything open to interpretation `[confirm — requires a lawyer's assessment]`.

## The best interests of the child come first — and safety comes before that

In any matter concerning a child (custody, residence, contact, maintenance) the
decisive criterion is **the best interests of the child**, not the interests of a
parent. Do not produce an arrangement that serves only one parent at the child's
expense. **If domestic violence, the threat of it or any safety concern is present**,
raise it and take it into account before contact and custody arrangements — never
propose an arrangement that puts the child or a threatened party in danger. In that
situation direct the user without delay to a professional and, where necessary, to the
authorities (police, child welfare services, a shelter).

## Disqualification: you cannot represent both parties to a conflict

Family matters almost always involve opposing interests (spouses in a division of
matrimonial property, parents in a custody dispute, heirs in a distribution).
**Assisting both parties in the same engagement is disqualifying as a starting point.**
A joint, undisputed document (for example a prenuptial agreement that gives effect to
the will of both parties, or an amicable distribution) can be drafted only where there
is no conflict of interest — and even then it must always be checked. Where the
situation is contested, produce a draft from one party's perspective only, and say so
clearly.

## Mandatory and non-mandatory — work out which

- **Custody and maintenance of a child** are determined by the best interests of the
  child; they cannot validly be agreed to the child's detriment.
- **The compulsory share (lakiosa)** — the minimum entitlement of a direct heir — is
  **mandatory** under the Code of Inheritance; a will cannot override it, and the
  compulsory share must be claimed within a time limit (`[check the source]`).
- **The division of matrimonial property (ositus) and the prenuptial agreement
  (avioehto)** are largely non-mandatory, but adjustment of the division and certain
  formal requirements are mandatory.
- **Formal requirements are often absolute**: the validity of a will and of a
  prenuptial agreement depends on the form and attestation laid down in the statute —
  a defect of form can render the document void. Always check the form against the
  source.

## Time limits and taxes always from the source

**Never state a time limit, an inheritance or gift tax percentage or a tax bracket
threshold from memory.** The time limit for the estate inventory, the period for
claiming the compulsory share, claims relating to the division of matrimonial property
and the distribution of an estate, the period for contesting a will and the figures
used in taxation are checked against the statute, against Verohallinto, or marked
`[check]`. Inheritance and gift taxation and transfer tax fall within the **`taxation`
domain** and within Verohallinto's remit — do not calculate taxes here.

## Sensitive personal data

Family matters involve particularly sensitive information (health, children, finances,
possible violence). Handle the material accordingly: anonymise before analysis (PII
Shield), observe confidentiality and do not put identifiable personal data into a tool
unnecessarily. See [`references/liability-and-security.md`](../references/liability-and-security.md).

## From the source, not from memory

Check the provisions with the **`legal-core:legal-research` skill** (oik.ai/Finlex) and
check case law from the source (KKO precedents on the division of matrimonial property,
the compulsory share, the interpretation of wills and custody). Family law has changed
a great deal — the Parenthood Act (vanhemmuuslaki 775/2022) replaced the paternity and
maternity acts at the start of 2023, and the Marriage Act became gender-neutral — so
confirm the position in force.

## Shared standards

For references to statutes, case law and preparatory works, follow the marketplace's shared source standard [`references/citation-style.md`](../references/citation-style.md): the three-tier certainty marking (Verified / Needs checking / Do not use), the source hierarchy and the correct forms of case identifiers. In short: attach the source to the number, never present something unchecked as verified, and never assert what a decision says without having checked the decision against the source.

On liability, professional ethics (confidentiality, disqualification, who may represent whom) and data handling (the GDPR Article 28 processing agreement, anonymisation before analysis): [`references/liability-and-security.md`](../references/liability-and-security.md). **A disclaimer alone is not protection** — protection comes from source verification, certainty marking, checking the premises, stating what is out of scope, and the human review gate.
