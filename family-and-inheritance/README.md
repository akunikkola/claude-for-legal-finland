# Family and inheritance law

Finnish family and inheritance law in everyday situations: the property relations of
marriage and the division of matrimonial property (ositus), the position of a cohabiting
partner, the custody, residence, contact and maintenance of a child, succession and wills,
and guardianship together with planning ahead for it.

> **Drafts and calculations are for review – not legal advice.**
> The best interests of the child decide matters concerning a child, and a safety concern
> comes before any arrangement. Formal requirements, time limits and taxes are always
> taken from the source, and both parties to a conflict cannot be represented. Inheritance
> and gift tax belong to the `taxation` domain. See [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md).

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| **marriage-and-division-of-property** | Prenuptial agreement (avioehtosopimus), the division of matrimonial property and the separation of assets on divorce and on death (avioliittolaki 234/1929), calculating the marital share and the equalisation payment (tasinko), adjustment of the division, and the dissolution of a cohabiting couple's household together with compensation (26/2011). Includes the reference on the fundamentals of family and inheritance law. |
| **child-status-and-maintenance** | Custody, residence, alternating residence and contact based on the best interests of the child (361/1983), assessment of child maintenance under the Ministry of Justice guideline and the maintenance agreement (704/1975), and the establishment of parenthood (vanhemmuuslaki 775/2022). A safety concern comes before any arrangement. |
| **inheritance-and-wills** | Order of succession, the position of a surviving spouse, drafting and interpreting a will, the compulsory share (lakiosa) of a direct heir, the estate inventory, administration of the estate and distribution of the inheritance (perintökaari 40/1965). Includes the reference on the fundamentals of the Code of Inheritance. |
| **guardianship-and-powers-of-attorney** | The anticipatory continuing power of attorney (648/2007) and guardianship already in force (holhoustoimilaki 442/1999): assessing the need, the guardian's duties, acts requiring authorisation, the duty to account, and the substitute guardian required by a conflict of interest. |

## Statutes verified as the basis

Verified against the source (Finlex): **avioliittolaki (234/1929)**, **laki
avopuolisoiden yhteistalouden purkamisesta (26/2011)**, **laki lapsen huollosta
ja tapaamisoikeudesta (361/1983)**, **laki lapsen elatuksesta (704/1975)**,
**vanhemmuuslaki (775/2022)** — which repealed the paternity act (11/2015) and the
maternity act (253/2018) as of 1 January 2023 —, **perintökaari (40/1965)**,
**laki holhoustoimesta (442/1999)** and **laki edunvalvontavaltuutuksesta
(648/2007)**. The wording of the sections, the time limits and the calculation of the
compulsory share are checked in use with the `legal-core:legal-research` skill.
