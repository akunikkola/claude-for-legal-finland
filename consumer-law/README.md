# Consumer law

Finnish consumer law in sales between a trader and a consumer: liability for defects in goods and
services, the right of withdrawal in distance and off-premises selling, the lawfulness of
marketing, and the resolution of consumer disputes and good collection practice for a consumer
debt.

> **Drafts are for review – not legal advice.**
> The Consumer Protection Act is **mandatory in the consumer's favour**: a term that weakens the
> consumer's statutory rights is void. The law changes frequently through EU directives, so the
> chapters, the time limits and the maximum collection charges are always taken from the source.
> See [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md).

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| **consumer-sales-liability** | Assessing a defect in goods or a service and the consumer's remedies in their proper order (repair or replacement, price reduction, cancellation of the sale, damages), the notice of defect, and the relationship between a guarantee and statutory liability for defects (kuluttajansuojalaki 38/1978). Includes a reference on the fundamentals of consumer law. |
| **distance-selling-and-withdrawal** | Pre-contractual information in distance and off-premises selling, the consumer's right of withdrawal and its exceptions, the withdrawal form and instructions, and assessing unfair and misleading marketing. |
| **consumer-disputes-and-collection** | The channels for resolving a dispute, from consumer advisory services to the Consumer Disputes Board (8/2007) and the courts, and good collection practice for a consumer debt, the collection charges and the payment demand (513/1999), and default interest (korkolaki 633/1982). |

## Statutes verified as the basis

Verified at source (Finlex): the **Consumer Protection Act (kuluttajansuojalaki 38/1978)**, the
**Act on the Consumer Disputes Board (laki kuluttajariitalautakunnasta 8/2007)**, the **Act on the
Finnish Competition and Consumer Authority (laki Kilpailu- ja kuluttajavirastosta 661/2012)**, the
**Debt Collection Act (laki saatavien perinnästä 513/1999)** and the **Interest Act (korkolaki
633/1982)**. The content of the chapters, the time limits and the maximum collection charges are
checked in use with the `legal-core:legal-research` skill and from kkv.fi.

## Related

- **the `contracts` domain** – B2B sales and sales between private individuals (the Sale of Goods
  Act, general contract law); the Consumer Protection Act does not apply to those.
- **the `insolvency` domain** – debt collection, limitation and enforcement generally.
- **the `dispute-resolution` domain** – taking a dispute to the district court.
- **the `data-protection` domain** – direct marketing and the processing of personal data.
- **the `legal-core` domain** – `legal-research` (the practice of the Board and of KKO).
