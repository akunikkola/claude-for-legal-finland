# Consumer law — shared guardrails and operating principles

The instructions in this file apply to **every** skill in this domain. SKILL.md says
*what* to do; this file is the **safety net**.

> **Design principle:** consumer law doctrine belongs in SKILL.md and in the references. These guardrails are life insurance.

---

## A draft that a human reviews

Notices of defect, replies, standard terms and complaints are **drafts for review** — not legal
advice. A binding decision or recommendation comes from the competent body: the Consumer Disputes
Board (kuluttajariitalautakunta), the Consumer Ombudsman (kuluttaja-asiamies, KKV) or a court. The
document is sent and signed by a human.

## The Consumer Protection Act is mandatory in the consumer's favour

The provisions of the Consumer Protection Act (kuluttajansuojalaki 38/1978) are **mandatory in the
consumer's favour**: a contract term that weakens the consumer's statutory rights is **void**
against them. This is the core of the domain. When you draft terms or a reply for a trader, you
cannot contract away the consumer's statutory rights; when you assist a consumer, their minimum
rights follow directly from the law regardless of the contract.

## Scope: a consumer or a trader?

The Act covers contracts between a trader and a **consumer** (a natural person acquiring goods
mainly for a purpose other than business). Always establish first whether this is a consumer sale:

- **B2C** → the Consumer Protection Act (this domain).
- **B2B or a sale between private individuals** → general contract law and the Sale of Goods Act
  (kauppalaki 355/1987) → the `contracts` domain. Do not apply the Consumer Protection Act to these.

## The law changes — EU directives keep rewriting chapters

The Consumer Protection Act has been reformed extensively through EU directives (among others the
reforms on the sale of goods, on digital content and services, and the omnibus reform). **The
content and numbering of the chapters have changed** — do not rely on a remembered chapter
structure; check the content in force at source.

## Time limits and maximum charges always from source

**Do not state a withdrawal period, a period for giving notice of a defect, the duration of a
guarantee or the maximum debt collection charges from memory.** The length of the right of
withdrawal, the reasonable time for giving notice of a defect, the months or years involved and
the statutory maximum collection charges for a consumer debt are checked in the law or marked
`[check]`; some of them are mandatory.

## Good debt collection practice

In the collection of a consumer debt (laki saatavien perinnästä 513/1999), **good collection
practice** must be followed: no unwarranted or unreasonable charges, no pressure, no collection of
a disputed or time-barred debt without proper basis. The maximum collection charges and the
content requirements for a payment demand are laid down in law. **Do not draft aggressive or
misleading collection** — refuse, and set out what good collection practice requires.

## From source, not from memory

Check the provisions with the **`legal-core:legal-research` skill** (oik.ai/Finlex), and the
decision practice of the Consumer Disputes Board and the positions of the Consumer Ombudsman at
source. The guidance of KKV and the Consumer Ombudsman is an authority source (kkv.fi) —
distinguish the law from authority practice.

## Shared standards

For statute, case-law and preparatory-works references, follow the collection's shared source standard [`references/citation-style.md`](../references/citation-style.md): three-tier certainty marking (Verified / Needs checking / Do not use), the source hierarchy and the correct forms of case identifiers. In short: attach the source to the number, never present something unchecked as verified, and never assert what a decision holds without having checked the decision at source.

On liability, professional ethics (confidentiality, disqualification, who may represent a client) and data handling (the GDPR Article 28 processing agreement, anonymisation before analysis): [`references/liability-and-security.md`](../references/liability-and-security.md). **A disclaimer alone is not protection** — protection comes from source verification, certainty marking, checking the premises, stating the negative scope and the human review gate.

## Practice profile (optional)

An organisation's standard terms, its returns and complaints practices, or sector-specific
positions can be stored here under the heading `## Practice profile`. A house position cannot
weaken the consumer's mandatory rights. Empty by default — do not invent anything; ask the user.
