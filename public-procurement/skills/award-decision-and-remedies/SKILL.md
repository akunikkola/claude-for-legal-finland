# The award decision and the remedies

This skill covers the closing stage of a procurement: the decision, the standstill
period, the conclusion of the contract, procurement rectification and the Market Court
process — from both points of view. The section headings 123 §, 128–129 §, 132–133 § and
146 § have been verified at source. Fundamentals:
`../procurement-planning/references/procurement-fundamentals.md`.

Drafts may be produced in **English** for review, but the version actually **filed with the court

documents). An English filing is not admissible. Always offer to produce the Finnish version, and

Keep the Finnish term alongside the English one for legally operative concepts on first use, for

## For the contracting authority: the decision and the contract

1. **The award decision (123 §)** — a written, reasoned decision on all the rulings
   affecting the position of the tenderers. The reasons must show the result of the
   comparison and the reasons for it: the scores, the written reasons by award criterion,
   and the advantages of the winner relative to the others. Weak reasons are a common
   ground for annulment — write so that an unsuccessful tenderer understands the reasons
   for its placing.
2. **Notification** with the appeal instructions (electronic notification; the
   instructions for appeal to the Market Court and for procurement rectification).
3. **The standstill period (129 §)** — in procurements above the EU threshold the
   contract may not be concluded before the standstill period ends `[check the length at
   source]`; an appeal to the Market Court prevents the conclusion of the contract
   automatically (the automatic suspension — the exceptions from the source).
4. **The procurement contract (128 §)** — it comes into being only on a written
   contract, not on notification of the decision. Its content = the content tendered.

## Procurement rectification (132–133 §)

- The contracting authority may **correct its own erroneous decision** — on its own
  initiative or at the request of a party; the time limits from the source.
- For a tenderer: a request for procurement rectification is quick and cheap, but it
  **does not extend the period for appealing to the Market Court** — do both in parallel
  if the interest is large.
- Procurement rectification does not require the consent of a party, but the conclusion
  of the contract restricts its use — check the limits at source.

## For the tenderer: an appeal to the Market Court (146 §)

1. **Assess the ground** — an error in the procedure: a discriminatory requirement, a
   comparison on criteria not announced, an error in the reasons, a wrong ruling on
   compliance with the invitation to tender, an unlawful direct award.
2. **Assess the interest** — a realistic chance of winning in a procedure without the
   error (the condition for a compensatory payment); the cost risk and the court fee; the
   business relationship with the contracting authority.
3. **The time limit** `[check at source — the main rule and the exceptions]`; an
   appeal triggers the automatic suspension before the contract.
4. **The claims**: the primary ones (annulment of the decision, correction of the error,
   a prohibition) and the secondary ones (a compensatory payment) — the secondary ones
   must be claimed at once; the ineffectiveness sanction and the other sanctions in an
   unlawful direct award (the conditions from the source).
5. **The structure of the submission**: the decision and the procurement identified, the
   errors set out concretely and tied to the invitation to tender and the decision, the
   evidence, the claims, and the claims concerning enforcement.

A further appeal to KHO requires leave to appeal →
`administrative-law:administrative-appeal` for the general doctrine of the process.

## Amendments during the contract period

A procurement contract may not be **materially amended** without a new call for tenders.
Structure the amendment: the permitted grounds for amendment (among others review clauses
written into the contract, additional orders, unforeseeable circumstances — the euro and
percentage limits from the source) as against a material amendment (a change in the
overall nature, a significant increase in price, a new supplier). Document the ground; an
unlawful amendment is a direct award, with the sanctions that follow.

## What this skill does NOT do

- **It does not confirm appeal periods, the length of the standstill period or the euro
  amounts of the sanctions from memory** — from the source or `[check]`.
- **It does not lodge an appeal or a request for rectification** — a human signs
  and is responsible for the time limit.
- **It does not predict the outcome in the Market Court** — it structures the grounds
  and the risks `[confirm — requires a procurement lawyer's assessment]`.
- **It does not draft ostensible reasons** for an erroneous decision — if
  there is an error in the comparison, the right advice is procurement rectification.
- **It does not deal with failures of performance during the contract period** (delay,
  defect) — those are contract disputes → `contracts` and `dispute-resolution`.
