# Public procurement

The whole arc of public procurement under the Public Procurement Act (1397/2016) – from
the point of view of both the contracting authority and the tenderer: planning and the
choice of procedure, the invitation to tender and the tender, and the award decision and
appeals.

> **Drafts are for review – not legal advice.**
> The thresholds and the time limits change; they are always taken from the source. The
> standstill period and the appeal periods are preclusive – responsibility for the
> calendar rests with a human.
> See [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md).

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| **procurement-planning** | Scope of application and thresholds (from the source), calculating the estimated value and the prohibition on splitting, market consultation, the choice of procedure (open, restricted, negotiated procedures, framework arrangement, the narrow grounds for a direct award) and publication in Hilma. Includes a reference on the fundamentals of procurement. |
| **tender-documents-and-bids** | For the contracting authority: the structure of the invitation to tender, suitability requirements versus award criteria, the most economically advantageous tender. For the tenderer: analysing the invitation to tender, a compliant tender, consortia and markings of trade secrets. |
| **award-decision-and-remedies** | The award decision and its reasons (123 §), the standstill period (129 §) and the conclusion of the contract (128 §), procurement rectification (132–133 §), an appeal to the Market Court (146 §) with its sanctions, and the limits on contract amendments during performance. |

## Statutes verified as the basis

Verified at source (Finlex): the **Act on Public Procurement and Concession Contracts
(laki julkisista hankinnoista ja käyttöoikeussopimuksista 1397/2016)** – the section
headings 123 § the decision on a procurement, 128 § the conclusion of the procurement
contract, 129 § the standstill period, 132–133 § procurement rectification, and 146 §
the subject of appeal and its restrictions – the **Act on Procurement and Concession
Contracts of Entities Operating in the Water, Energy, Transport and Postal Services
Sectors (1398/2016)** and the **Act on Public Defence and Security Procurement
(1531/2011)**. The thresholds and other euro amounts are checked in use at source
(Finlex, Hilma, the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment).
