# Company law — shared guardrails and operating principles

The instructions in this file apply to **every** skill in this domain. SKILL.md says
*what* to do; this file is the **safety net**.

> **Design principle:** company-law doctrine belongs in SKILL.md and in the references. These
> guardrails are the life insurance.

---

## A draft that a human reviews

Drafts of articles of association (yhtiöjärjestys), agreements and minutes, and the sequencing of
transactions, are **drafts for review** — not legal advice. Corporate acts (notices of meeting,
resolutions, filings to the trade register, signatures) are carried out by a human, who is
responsible for them. Mark anything open to interpretation
`[confirm — requires a company-law lawyer's assessment]`.

## Freedom of contract is not unlimited

The Limited Liability Companies Act (osakeyhtiölaki 624/2006) is largely **non-mandatory**, but its
mandatory core cannot be contracted away: **protection of creditors, equal treatment of
shareholders (OYL 1:7) and minority protection**. Do not draft articles of association, an
agreement or a structure that circumvents these — even if every current party "agrees to it". Raise
the risk.

## The two-part distributable-funds test

In every distribution of assets (dividend, repayment from the reserve for invested unrestricted
equity (SVOP), acquisition of own shares, group contribution) check **both**: the balance-sheet test
(distributable funds, OYL chapter 13) and the **solvency test** (OYL 13:2, verified: assets may not
be distributed if, at the time the distribution is decided on, it is known or ought to be known that
the company is insolvent or that the distribution will cause insolvency). An unlawful distribution
gives rise to liability to return the assets and to liability in damages.

## From the source, not from memory — and watch for amendments

Check provisions **with the `legal-core:legal-research` skill** (oik.ai/Finlex). In particular:

- **The Trade Register Act is 564/2023** (confirmed from the source) — it replaced the old trade
  register act (kaupparekisterilaki 129/1979). Do not cite the old one as being in force.
- **A private limited company has no minimum share capital** (the requirement was removed in 2019);
  the minimum share capital of a public limited company is 80 000 euros (OYL, confirmed).
- **Cross-border transactions are governed by OYL chapter 17 a** (added by act 1337/2022).
- Do not state **time limits, notice periods for general meetings, the stages of the
  creditor-protection procedure or merger-control turnover thresholds from memory** — they have
  changed and will change again. Retrieve them from the source or mark them `[check]`.

## A shareholders' agreement and the articles of association are different instruments

The articles of association (yhtiöjärjestys) bind the company, its governing bodies and all
shareholders as a matter of company law; a shareholders' agreement (osakassopimus) binds **only the
parties to it** as a matter of contract law. When the same matter appears in both, or in only one of
them, check for conflicts and explain the difference in enforcement (challenge or invalidity of a
resolution versus a contractual penalty or damages).

## Tax is its own discipline

The tax treatment of corporate transactions and distributions (business income tax, income tax,
transfer tax) often decides the choice of structure, but **do not present a tax treatment as
settled**. Mark tax points `[confirm — requires a tax specialist's assessment]` and, where
appropriate, suggest applying for an advance ruling.

## Shared standards

For references to statutes, case law and preparatory works, follow the collection's shared source
standard in [`references/citation-style.md`](../references/citation-style.md): three-tier certainty
marking (Verified / Needs checking / Do not use), the source hierarchy, and the correct forms of
case identifiers. In short: attach the source to the number, never present something unchecked as
verified, and never assert what a decision says without having checked the decision from the source.

On liability, professional ethics (confidentiality, disqualification, who may represent whom) and
data handling (GDPR Article 28 processing agreement, anonymisation before analysis):
[`references/liability-and-security.md`](../references/liability-and-security.md). **A disclaimer
alone is not protection** — protection comes from verifying the source, certainty marking, checking
the premises, stating the negative scope, and the human review gate.
