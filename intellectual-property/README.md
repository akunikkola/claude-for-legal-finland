# Intellectual property

Finnish intellectual property law for protecting and defending a company's intangible
assets: trade marks and trade names, copyright and trade secrets – registration
strategy, infringement assessment, licensing and the links to contracts.

> **Assessments and drafts are for review – not legal advice.**
> The domain cannot see the registers (PRH, EUIPO, WIPO) and therefore never confirms that a
> mark is free or that an invention is new – it produces a search strategy for a human.
> See [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md).

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| **trademarks-and-trade-names** | Protection of a trade mark (544/2019) and a trade name (128/1979): registration strategy (PRH/EUIPO/Madrid), distinctiveness, assessment of the likelihood of confusion, collisions between trade name, trade mark and domain name, and the options in an infringement situation. Includes a reference on the fundamentals of IP. |
| **copyright** | Copyright (404/1961, the DSM reforms verified): the threshold of originality, economic and moral rights, related rights, rights arising in employment and commissioning relationships (including 40 b § on computer programs), licensing and permitted use. |
| **trade-secrets** | The Trade Secrets Act (595/2018): what counts as a trade secret, reasonable protective measures, infringement situations and remedies, the employee dimension and the link between NDAs and contracts. The strategic choice between secrecy and patenting. |

## Statutes verified as the basis

Verified at source (Finlex): the **Trade Marks Act (tavaramerkkilaki 544/2019)**, the **Copyright
Act (tekijänoikeuslaki 404/1961)** – which contains the amendments implementing the DSM Directive
(online content-sharing services, text and data mining) and 40 b § on the transfer of computer
programs created in employment – the **Trade Secrets Act (liikesalaisuuslaki 595/2018)**, the
**Patents Act (patenttilaki 550/1967)**, the **Registered Designs Act (mallioikeuslaki
221/1971)**, the **Utility Models Act (laki hyödyllisyysmallioikeudesta 800/1991)**, the **Trade
Names Act (toiminimilaki 128/1979)**, the **Unfair Business Practices Act (laki sopimattomasta
menettelystä elinkeinotoiminnassa 1061/1978)** and the **Act on the Right in Employee Inventions
(laki oikeudesta työntekijän tekemiin keksintöihin 656/1967)**. The content of individual sections
is checked in use with the `legal-core:legal-research` skill.
