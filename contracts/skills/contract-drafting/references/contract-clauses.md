# Contract clauses — annotated models

Model clauses for the most common parts of a contract. **These are starting points, not finished
solutions** — adapt them to the type of contract, the positions of the parties and the risks.
Check the legislative references with the `legal-research` skill. Square brackets `[ ]` mark
what has to be completed.

## The parties

```
This contract has been made between:

[Official name of the company] (business ID [0000000-0]), hereinafter the "Supplier", and
[Official name of the company] (business ID [0000000-0]), hereinafter the "Customer",

hereinafter together the "Parties" and separately a "Party".
```

## Subject matter of the contract

```
The Supplier shall supply to the Customer [description of the service/goods] in accordance
with this contract and its annexes. The scope of and requirements for the supply are defined
in Annex [1].
```

## Price and payment terms

```
The price under the contract is [amount] euros (VAT 0 %). Value added tax is added at the
rate in force at the time. The Supplier shall invoice [basis of invoicing]. The payment
period is [14] days from the date of the invoice. Interest on late payment is determined
under the Interest Act (korkolaki).
```
*Note: check the basis of the interest on late payment in korkolaki with the `legal-research`
skill.*

## Confidentiality

```
A Party shall not disclose to a third party, or use for any purpose other than the purpose
under this contract, confidential information received from the other Party. The
confidentiality obligation does not cover information that is generally available, that the
Party has lawfully received from a third party without an obligation of confidentiality, or
that the Party has independently developed. The confidentiality obligation remains in force
for [5] years after the termination of the contract.
```

## Liability and limitation of liability

```
A Party is liable to the other Party for direct loss caused to it by a breach of contract. A
Party is not liable for indirect loss, such as lost profit or loss of production. A Party's
total liability under this contract is limited to [the value of the contract / an amount of]
euros.

The limitations of liability do not cover loss that a Party has caused intentionally or by
gross negligence, nor a breach of the confidentiality obligation.
```
*Note: limiting liability in respect of intent or gross negligence is not valid — keep the
exception in.*

## Contractual penalty

```
If a Party breaches [specified obligation, e.g. the confidentiality obligation], it shall be
liable to pay the other Party a contractual penalty of [amount] euros for each breach. In
addition to the contractual penalty, compensation may be claimed for the part of the
substantiated loss that exceeds the contractual penalty.
```
*Note: an unreasonable contractual penalty may be exposed to adjustment (section 36 of
oikeustoimilaki 228/1929).*

## Force majeure

```
A Party is released from its obligations and from liability if performance of the contract is
prevented or delayed by an impediment beyond the Party's control which the Party could not
reasonably have been expected to take into account when the contract was made (for example a
natural disaster, an order of an authority, a general interruption of traffic or of the energy
supply). A Party shall notify the other Party of the impediment without delay.
```

## Term and termination

```
The contract enters into force when both Parties have signed it and is in force [until
further notice / for a fixed term until [dd.mm.yyyy]]. Either Party may terminate the
contract on [3] months' notice. A Party may rescind the contract with immediate effect if
the other Party is in material breach of the contract and does not cure the breach within
[30] days of a written notice.
```

## Applicable law and dispute resolution

```
This contract is governed by Finnish law.

[Option A — the general courts:]
Disputes arising from this contract shall be resolved in the District Court of [locality].

[Option B — arbitration:]
Disputes arising from this contract shall be finally resolved by arbitration in accordance
with the Arbitration Rules of the Finland Chamber of Commerce. The seat of the arbitral
tribunal is [Helsinki] and the language of the proceedings is Finnish.
```

## Other terms (miscellaneous)

```
Amendments to the contract must be made in writing and signed by both Parties. A Party may
not assign the contract to a third party without the written consent of the other Party. The
annexes to the contract form an integral part of it; in the event of conflict the text of the
contract takes precedence over the annexes. This contract supersedes the Parties' earlier
contracts and agreements on the same matter.
```
