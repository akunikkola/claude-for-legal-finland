# Banking and finance

Finnish banking and finance law for companies and their advisers: finance
agreements and collateral, preventing money laundering, and securities markets
regulation.

> **Drafts are for review – not legal advice, and not investment advice.**
> Financial regulation is EU-driven and changes quickly – the provisions,
> interest rate caps and thresholds are always fetched from the source. No
> assistance is given with the misuse of inside information or with money
> laundering in any form.
> See [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md).

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| **financing-and-collateral** | Structuring finance agreements and collateral: the promissory note (622/1947) and the loan terms (covenants, acceleration), interest and default interest (633/1982), guarantees and third-party pledges (361/1999, the mandatory protection of a private guarantor), pledges, business mortgages and other security, and the collateral package as a whole. Includes the finance law fundamentals reference. |
| **anti-money-laundering** | The Anti-Money Laundering Act (rahanpesulaki 444/2017): scope (including legal services), customer due diligence and the obligation to obtain information, beneficial owners, the risk assessment, reporting a suspicious transaction and the prohibition on disclosure – building the compliance programme for an obliged entity. |
| **securities-markets** | The Securities Markets Act (arvopaperimarkkinalaki 746/2012) and MAR: a listed company's disclosure obligation, managing inside information (disclosure, delay, lists), managers' transactions, notification of major holdings, and the basics of prospectus regulation and takeover bids. |

## Statutes verified as the basis

Verified from the source (Finlex): the **Promissory Notes Act (velkakirjalaki
622/1947)**, the **Act on Guarantees and Third-Party Pledges (laki takauksesta
ja vierasvelkapanttauksesta 361/1999)**, the **Interest Act (korkolaki
633/1982)**, the **Act on Preventing Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing
(laki rahanpesun ja terrorismin rahoittamisen estämisestä 444/2017)**, the
**Securities Markets Act (arvopaperimarkkinalaki 746/2012)** and the **Act on
Credit Institutions (laki luottolaitostoiminnasta 610/2014)**. The EU level
(MAR, MiFID II, the Prospectus Regulation, the AML package) is checked in use
from EUR-Lex and from Finanssivalvonta's guidance.
