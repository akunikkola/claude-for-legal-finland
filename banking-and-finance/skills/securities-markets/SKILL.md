# Securities markets — disclosure, inside information and transactions

This skill structures the securities markets obligations of a listed
company and its advisers. The regulatory basis: the Securities Markets
Act (arvopaperimarkkinalaki 746/2012) + directly applicable EU regulation
(MAR, the Prospectus Regulation) + Fiva's regulations and the rules of
Nasdaq Helsinki. Fundamentals:
`../financing-and-collateral/references/finance-fundamentals.md`.

> **Disclaimer:** drafts are for review — not legal advice and not
> investment advice. **No assistance is given with the misuse of inside
> information** — an absolute negative scope (AGENTS.md). The sanctions
> are severe: administrative penalty payments and criminal liability.

## Check the regulatory layers from the source

Three layers, check all of them: **AML 746/2012** (Finlex), the **EU
regulations** (MAR, the Prospectus Regulation — EUR-Lex, including
ESMA's guidelines) and **Fiva's regulations and guidelines and the
exchange's rules** `[check]`. Use the
`legal-core:legal-research` skill for national law; the EU texts and the
thresholds from the source.

## Managing inside information (MAR)

1. **Is the information inside information?** Precise, not made public,
   likely to have a significant effect on the price — the assessment is
   documented in every project (the reasonable investor test). Where
   uncertain, treat it as inside information.
2. **Disclosure as soon as possible** — the main rule; the content
   requirements for a stock exchange release (materiality, consistency
   with earlier communications).
3. **Delay** — the conditions (a legitimate interest, no misleading of
   the public, confidentiality assured) and the procedure (documenting
   the decision to delay, notifying Fiva on publication) — the details
   from the source.
4. **Insider lists** — project-specific; keeping them up to date,
   acknowledgements of awareness; permanent insiders are a separate
   matter.
5. **A leak of information** → immediate disclosure; have a policy on
   commenting on rumours ready in advance.

## Managers' transactions and trading restrictions

- **Transaction notifications** by managers and their closely associated
  persons (the time limits and the threshold from the source) to the
  company and to Fiva + publication.
- **The closed window** before results announcements (its length from the
  source) — the company's internal trading policy is often wider; draft
  the policy.

## Regular and other disclosure obligations

- The financial statements, the half-year report and any interim reports
  — the requirements from AML and from the exchange's rules `[check]`.
- Consistency: a profit warning when the guidance no longer holds —
  assessing the threshold and the structure of the release.
- **Notification of major holdings** (AML): notifying significant
  holdings — the thresholds and the calculation (including financial
  instruments) from the source; the obligations of both the notifying
  party and the company.

## Transactions

- **The prospectus** — when it is required and the exemptions (the
  Prospectus Regulation, the thresholds from the source); the national
  basic information document in small offerings `[check]`. Due diligence
  for a listing → the `company-law:corporate-transactions` framework +
  the market-specific parts.
- **A public takeover bid** (AML + the Helsinki Takeover Code): the
  mandatory bid threshold, the consideration and the offer document,
  equal treatment — the details from the source; the Finnish Securities
  Market Association's Takeover Code as a comply-or-explain basis.
- **Market soundings** (MAR) — the permitted procedure before a
  transaction: consents, documentation, cleansing.

## What this skill does NOT do

- **Does not assist with insider dealing, with unlawful disclosure of
  information or with manipulation** — an absolute refusal; a suspicious
  request → escalate to compliance.
- **Does not assess the price effect of information with binding
  effect** — the materiality assessment belongs to the company and its
  advisers `[confirm]`.
- **Does not confirm time limits, notification thresholds or prospectus
  thresholds from memory** — from the source or `[check]`.
- **Does not draft or publish stock exchange releases** — a draft goes
  into the company's process (the board or the disclosure committee).
- **Does not give investment recommendations**.
