# Information sources and search guidance

This guide explains where to find the material needed to draft a statement and how to search for
it efficiently.

## Primary sources

### Lausuntopalvelu.fi

**What it is:** a service maintained by the government that collects consultation rounds together.
Statements are given and published here.

**What you will find:**
- Open consultation rounds and their deadlines
- The text of the request for statements and its annexes (usually the draft bill)
- Statements already given by others (often published after the round closes)

**Search guidance:**
- URL: `https://www.lausuntopalvelu.fi`
- You can search open rounds or closed ones
- Search terms: the name of the act (e.g. "vesihuoltolaki"), the name of the ministry, the subject area
- WebSearch query: `site:lausuntopalvelu.fi [name of the act] lausuntopyyntö`

### Finlex.fi

**What it is:** the public and free online service of legal material maintained by the Ministry of
Justice.

**What you will find:**
- Legislation in force (acts and decrees)
- Government bills (HE)
- Case law (KKO, KHO)
- Authority norms

**Search guidance:**
- Legislation in force: `https://www.finlex.fi/fi/laki/ajantasa/`
- Search by the number of the act: `https://www.finlex.fi/fi/laki/ajantasa/YEAR/YEARNUMBER`
  - E.g. vesihuoltolaki: `https://www.finlex.fi/fi/laki/ajantasa/2001/20010119`
- Government bills: `https://www.finlex.fi/fi/esitykset/he/`
- WebSearch query: `site:finlex.fi [name of the act] ajantasa`

### Valtioneuvosto.fi

**What it is:** the government's website, where legislative projects are announced.

**What you will find:**
- Open consultation rounds (a central list)
- Project details and the progress of preparation
- Press releases on legislative reforms
- Draft bills

**Search guidance:**
- Consultation rounds: `https://valtioneuvosto.fi/hankkeet/osallistu-ja-vaikuta`
- Ministry-specific pages: `https://[ministry abbreviation].fi/`
  - E.g. the Ministry of the Environment: `https://ym.fi/`
  - The Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry: `https://mmm.fi/`
  - The Ministry of Finance: `https://vm.fi/`
- WebSearch query: `site:valtioneuvosto.fi [name of the act] lausuntokierros`

### Eduskunta.fi

**What it is:** the website of Parliament.

**What you will find:**
- Government bills already submitted to Parliament
- Committee reports and statements
- Plenary session minutes
- Details of expert hearings

**Search guidance:**
- Parliamentary business: `https://www.eduskunta.fi/FI/vaski/sivut/trip.aspx`
- HE search: search field → document type: HE
- WebSearch query: `site:eduskunta.fi HE [number]/[year]`

### Kuntaliitto.fi

**What it is:** the website of the Association of Finnish Municipalities (Suomen Kuntaliitto ry).
Kuntaliitto represents all Finnish municipalities.

**What you will find:**
- Kuntaliitto's position papers and statements on bills
- General guidance for municipalities
- Statistics on the municipal sector
- Current news from the municipal sector

**Search guidance:**
- URL: `https://www.kuntaliitto.fi/`
- Statements: `https://www.kuntaliitto.fi/lausunnot`
- WebSearch query: `site:kuntaliitto.fi [name of the act] lausunto`

## Secondary sources

### Pirkanmaan liitto

- URL: `https://www.pirkanmaa.fi/`
- Regional position papers and statements
- Relevant in particular to land-use and regional-development matters

### Tilastokeskus

- URL: `https://www.stat.fi/`
- Population data, economic statistics, municipality-level data
- Useful when you need figures to support the statement

### The Ministry of Finance — municipal finances

- URL: `https://vm.fi/kuntatalous`
- The state of municipal finances and forecasts
- Central government transfer calculations

### The Ministry of Justice — legislative drafting

- URL: `https://oikeusministerio.fi/lainvalmistelu`
- General guidance on legislative drafting
- The consultation guide: `https://kuulemisopas.finlex.fi/`

## Search strategies

### When you are looking for a particular bill

1. Start on Lausuntopalvelu.fi → look for open or closed rounds
2. If it is not there, search on Valtioneuvosto.fi under the ministry's pages
3. Check Eduskunta.fi if the HE has already been submitted to Parliament
4. WebSearch: `"[name of the act]" hallituksen esitys luonnos site:valtioneuvosto.fi OR site:lausuntopalvelu.fi`

### When you are looking for the act in force

1. Finlex.fi → legislation in force → search by the name or number of the act
2. WebSearch: `site:finlex.fi "[name of the act]" ajantasa`

### When you are looking for other municipalities' statements

1. Lausuntopalvelu.fi → find the closed round → browse the statements
2. WebSearch: `"[name of the act]" lausunto kunta site:lausuntopalvelu.fi`
3. Kuntaliitto.fi → look for Kuntaliitto's own statement
4. Individual municipalities' websites (less often available)

### When you are looking for impact assessments

1. Section 4.2 of the draft bill (the principal impacts)
2. Kuntaliitto's statement (often contains an assessment of the impacts on municipalities)
3. The Ministry of Finance's municipal finance calculations
4. Other municipalities' statements (impact assessments municipality by municipality)

## Storing the material

When you retrieve material from the web:
- Save the draft bill, or its material parts, into the workspace
- Record the source references (URL, date, name of the document)
- If the material is in PDF form, use the PDF skill to read it
- Summarise long documents before analysing them
