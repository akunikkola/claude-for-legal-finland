# Consultation statements

Drafting a consultation statement (lausunto) on a legislative reform or a draft act from any
respondent's point of view: municipality, city, joint municipal authority, government agency,
association or company.

> **A statement is a draft that the respondent's responsible officer reviews and approves.**
> Value judgements are left to the decision-makers. See [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md).

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| **consultation-statement-assistant** | A seven-stage workflow: scoping, gathering source material, analysing the bill, analysing other statements, writing the statement, checking it and submitting it. Eight references (analysis tools, impact assessment, structure, examples, writing guidelines, information sources, lausuntopalvelu guide, respondent profile template). |

## Respondent profile

`references/respondent-profile.md` is an empty template. Fill in your own organisation's details
(type, size, sector, resources, distinctive features) and impact assessments will be scaled to it.
You can also store the details permanently in the practice profile in [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md).

## Related

- **`legal-core` domain** — `legal-research` (retrieving the law in force and case law from oik.ai/Finlex, which this skill uses for comparison) and `document-review` (a thorough check of the finished statement).
- **`legislative-drafting` domain** — the drafting side; consultation statements are the other end of the same preparatory process.
