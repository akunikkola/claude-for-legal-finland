# The position of the injured party — claims and rights

This skill structures the rights of the injured party and drafts the claims.
Fundamentals: `../pre-trial-investigation-and-coercive-measures/references/criminal-procedure-fundamentals.md`.

> **Disclaimer:** the drafts are for counsel and the injured party to
> review — not legal advice. The amounts of compensation are justified
> from the sources, not from memory. See `criminal-procedure/AGENTS.md`.

## Output language

Drafts may be produced in **English** for review, but the version actually **filed with the court
or authority must be in Finnish or Swedish** (kielilaki 423/2003; oikeudenkäymiskaari for court
documents). An English filing is not admissible. Always offer to produce the Finnish version, and
state plainly that the English text is a working translation only.

Keep the Finnish term alongside the English one for legally operative concepts on first use, for
example `notice period (irtisanomisaika)`.

## Check the law at source

Look up the provisions of ROL on the injured party, the grounds for compensation in the
Tort Liability Act (412/1974), the conditions and time limits of the Criminal Injuries
Act (1204/2005), and the recommendations of the Advisory Board for Personal Injury
Matters and the compensation practice of KKO, with the **`legal-core:legal-research` skill**
and from official sources.

## Step 1: Position and strategy

- **A complainant offence or one subject to public prosecution?** — in a complainant
  offence the request for prosecution (and the consequences of withdrawing it) is
  decisive — check the right to prosecute for that offence at source.
- **What is the injured party seeking?** Compensation, a sentence, safety (a restraining
  order), or the lightest possible process — the strategy and the claims are calibrated
  to the objective.
- **Counsel and a support person**: the conditions for appointing counsel in the
  proceedings and a support person (among other things certain types of offence) and
  legal aid (257/2002) — check at source; make the questions of liability for costs clear
  at once.

## Step 2: Civil claims

1. **Put the claims forward in time** — itemised already during the investigation;
   ask **the prosecutor to pursue** a clear and simple claim (a request under
   ROL — the conditions from the source; free of charge to the injured party). A complex
   or disputed claim generally requires the injured party's own counsel.
2. **Types of compensation (412/1974)** — structure them separately:
   - personal injury (medical care, loss of earnings, pain and suffering, permanent
     handicap),
   - **suffering** (caused by the violation — the conditions from the source),
   - damage to property and financial loss,
   - the costs of investigating the matter and the legal costs.
3. **Justifying the amount** — receipts, medical statements, the recommendations of the
   Advisory Board for Personal Injury Matters and comparable decisions from the source
   `[check the level of award at source]`; interest on the claim (under the Interest Act
   — check).
4. **A claim in a judgment is not money in the account** — enforcement of the judgment
   through the enforcement authority (→ `insolvency:debt-collection`) and the next section.

## Step 3: Compensation under the Criminal Injuries Act (1204/2005)

Valtiokonttori may pay compensation to the victim of an offence out of state funds:

- Subsidiarity (insurance and any compensation awarded come first), the types of
  compensation and the exclusions, the deductible and the maximum amounts, and the
  **period for applying** — all from the source `[check]`.
- Draft the application: the judgment or the investigation material as an annex, and the
  documentation of the losses at the same time.

## Step 4: When the prosecutor does not prosecute

Responding to a decision not to prosecute:

1. **A request for reconsideration** to the Prosecutor General — in any form, with
   reasons; structure the new evidence or the error of assessment.
2. **The secondary right to prosecute** — the injured party may bring the charge
   themselves (the conditions of ROL from the source). Set out the realities: the burden
   of proof, the cost risk and liability for the other side's costs if unsuccessful —
   `[confirm — requires a criminal lawyer's assessment]` before bringing it.

## Protective measures

- **A restraining order (898/1998)** — the basic, extended and intra-family forms; the
  conditions, how it is applied for (the police or the district court) and the
  consequences of breaching it, from the source. Draft the application: the concrete
  events on a timeline, the evidence, the scope sought.
- A non-disclosure order and the protection of contact details in the proceedings; being
  heard behind a screen or by video (the conditions from the source).

## What this skill does NOT do

- **It does not promise an amount of compensation** — the level is taken from the
  recommendations and the practice at source; the court decides.
- **It does not act for the defendant and the injured party in the same case** —
  a conflict of interest (AGENTS.md).
- **It does not confirm application or appeal periods from memory** — from the source
  or `[check]`.
- **It does not press for a settlement or for giving up claims** — the decisions are the
  injured party's.
- **It does not replace victim support services** — direct the person where needed to
  Victim Support Finland (riku.fi) and to a legal aid office.
